Acai Travel · Traveltech · AI Product · 2025

Designing an AI agent for travel

AMA lets travel agents query fare rules and GDS manuals in seconds — replacing hours of manual lookup, without replacing their expertise.

RoleLead Product Designer
TypeInternal AI Tool
Timeline2025 · Ongoing
PlatformWeb App
Problem

Hours lost to manual knowledge work

Travel agents spent 5–30 minutes per query hunting through fare manuals, GDS docs, and Slack threads for answers that already existed — just scattered. Shadowing sessions and ticket analysis showed agents lost up to 40% of their day this way, and 80% of questions were variations of the same 20 scenarios.

"Agents didn't want to be replaced by AI — they wanted a knowledgeable colleague on demand."
Approach

Designing for trust, not just speed

An AI that gives wrong fare advice can cause real financial harm — so the core challenge wasn't the chat interface, it was making agents trust the answer enough to act on it.

01

Discovery

Shadowing sessions, ticket analysis, 12 agent interviews.

02

Framing

Defined the AI's voice — confident but citable. Scoped MVP queries.

03

Design

Iterated on conversation UI, source citations, suggested questions.

04

Validate

Weekly usability tests measuring trust, speed, and error rates.

Solution

From hours of search to seconds of certainty

Agents type a question in natural language and get a structured, source-cited answer in seconds — showing exactly which manual or GDS doc it came from. That single change is what earned their trust.

Before

  • 5–30 minutes per query
  • 3–5 browser tabs of manuals
  • Ask a senior colleague on Slack

After

  • Answer in under 5 seconds
  • Source cited inline
  • Full chat history, anytime
Outcomes

Faster answers, more confident agents
